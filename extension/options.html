TrueBeat: options

The extension sends captured video to this backend and shows what it answers. Nothing else is contacted.

A local backend needs no extra permission. For a hosted backend (e.g. an https://*.hf.space URL) the extension asks for access to that origin when you save.
12–60 s. Longer captures find the pulse better but take longer to analyse (roughly 20 s of processing per minute of video on a CPU).

Privacy

Captures are not stored by the extension and are sent only to the backend URL above. There is no telemetry, no analytics and no other network traffic. A local backend keeps everything on your machine.

This tool reads a face's blood-flow signal. Use it on video you are allowed to analyse, and remember that face videos are biometric data.
